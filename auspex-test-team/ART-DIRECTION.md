# Construções do Pátio de ferro

Novo material gerado com a ferramenta integrada de imagens (modo built-in, não CLI). Arquivo final: `assets/pixel/industrial-materials-v1.png`.

O atlas tem quatro materiais: parede blindada, mureta de alvenaria, caixote e piso metálico. O desenho é aplicado às faces reais de cada construção, preservando footprint, altura, portas, aberturas e quatro rotações. Piso do tabuleiro, operativos e terminais mantêm as artes existentes. Não usamos os sprites de cenário Metropole neste pacote.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art material texture atlas for a rotating isometric tactical game. Create one square image divided into EXACTLY four equal square quadrants, a 2 by 2 grid, no padding, no labels, no gutters. All four quadrants fill their entire tile. These are FLAT front-facing orthographic material surfaces, absolutely no perspective, no isometric view, no rendered cubes, no characters, no objects floating on a background. Top-left quadrant: weathered blue-grey industrial bunker wall, large irregular steel armor panels over dark stone masonry, rivets, inset dark ventilation grille, small worn ochre/brass clamps, rich deliberate pixel-art detail. Top-right quadrant: chipped dark blue-grey stone/concrete blocks for ruined parapets, a muted ochre armored trim across the top, cracked worn face, no holes through the material. Bottom-left quadrant: olive-grey armored supply-crate panel, strong angular steel reinforcement frame, bronze locks and hinges, small muted amber hardware, no text or symbols. Bottom-right quadrant: top-down industrial platform decking, square grey metal floor plates with recessed seams, sparse bronze bolts, restrained worn yellow hazard strip near one edge. Style: premium hand-pixelled 2D strategy-game environment texture, crisp square pixel clusters, hard stepped highlights, limited unified slate/navy/grey/brass palette, visible tasteful surface damage, not photorealistic and not smooth painting. Texture density equivalent to about 96 x 96 logical pixels per quadrant enlarged with nearest-neighbor pixels, with strong readable broad forms. Keep these same four materials evenly lit and complementary to dark steel battlefield floors and detailed blue armored pixel sprites. No cast shadows, no background margins, no sky, no writing, no watermarks, no emblems.
